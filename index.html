Why Do You Need an Online Mouse Jiggler?

In the era of remote work, communication tools like Microsoft Teams, Slack, Skype, and Zoom have aggressive "Away" status timers. Usually, if you don't move your mouse for 5 minutes, your status turns yellow ("Away"), signaling to colleagues and managers that you are not working.

This Online Mouse Jiggler solves that problem instantly. By keeping your system awake, it ensures your status remains green ("Available") while you take a quick break, brainstorm on paper, or focus on offline tasks.

How This Tool Works (Technical Breakdown)

Unlike physical mouse jigglers that vibrate your physical mouse, JigglerPro uses two advanced browser technologies:

  • Screen Wake Lock API: A modern standard that allows web pages to signal the Operating System to prevent dimming or locking.
  • Video Fallback Engine: For older browsers (like older Safari versions), we play a microscopic, invisible video loop. Most OSs will not sleep while a video is playing.
