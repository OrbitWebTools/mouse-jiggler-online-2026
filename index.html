  Start-Sleep -Seconds 59
}

What Is a Mouse Jiggler (And How Does an Online Mouse Jiggler Work)?

A mouse jiggler (also called a mouse mover or mouse wiggler) is a tool or device that keeps your computer awake and prevents your screen from locking during long downloads, presentations, live dashboards, or offline reading. Remote workers also use mouse jigglers to stop chat apps like Microsoft Teams, Slack, and Zoom from switching their status to yellow "Away" after 5 minutes of reading paper notes or taking a phone call.

Because web browsers enforce strict security sandboxes, no website can directly hijack your operating system's cursor. Instead of making misleading claims, OrbitWebTools Online Mouse Jiggler combines all three genuinely effective browser-safe methods in one free studio:

  • 1. Screen Wake Lock API: Sends a native wake signal directly to Windows, macOS, Android, or iOS so your display never dims or locks while viewing dashboards, code builds, or recipes.
  • 2. Optical Mouse DIY Mode (Phone Screen): Generates a high-contrast animated optical tracking pattern on your smartphone. Resting your PC's optical mouse on the phone screen triggers real, hardware-level cursor micro-movements—giving you the same result as a $30 Vaydeer or Tech8 mechanical turntable for free.
  • 3. Native OS Keep-Awake Scripts: Copy built-in Windows PowerShell (F15 virtual key) or macOS caffeinate commands directly from your terminal without downloading risky third-party .exe executables.

Keeping Microsoft Teams, Slack, Windows 11 & Mac Awake

Both Microsoft Teams and Slack monitor OS-level input idle timers (typically 5 minutes). Mouse Jiggler Detection: What IT & Employer Monitoring Actually Sees

Enterprise endpoint management tools (such as Microsoft Intune, CrowdStrike, and employee monitoring suites) typically flag mouse jigglers in three ways: scanning for known .exe process names (like MouseJiggler.exe), auditing USB hardware IDs plugged into the laptop, or analyzing unnaturally robotic cursor loops. Using a standard browser Wake Lock tab for legitimate dashboard viewing—or native OS tools like caffeinate on a personal Mac—avoids installing untrusted third-party binaries altogether.
